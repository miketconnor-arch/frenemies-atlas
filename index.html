Hold the printed square in view. The hologram is pinned to it; if the square leaves the picture, the hologram waits where it was.

One finger drag slides the atlas across the table. Pinch grows or shrinks it. Two-finger twist turns it. Tap a world to open its card; tap empty space to close it. Reset puts it back on the square.

The map lies flat; the higher a world floats, the later it was made. Size and brightness are the impact rating: the brightest are friends (+10), the cold blue ones enemies (−10). Works with no single date or place circle the outside ring.
